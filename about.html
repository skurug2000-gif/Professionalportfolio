Professional Bio

Who I am

I'm a security engineer with hands-on experience evaluating vendor risk, access control, and compliance requirements across frameworks like SOC 2, ISO 27001, and HIPAA. I'm currently building on that foundation in IWU's AIML program, where I'm learning to apply AI and machine learning directly to the security and GRC problems I work on day to day — not just as a user of AI tools, but as someone who designs and builds with them.

What draws me to this intersection is practical: security and compliance work is full of repetitive, judgment-heavy tasks — like drafting vendor questionnaires — that are perfect candidates for AI assistance, provided the tool is built with the right guardrails. This portfolio documents that growing skill set as I work through the AIML program.

Personal Value Proposition

What I bring

I bring a rare combination for AI/ML work in security: real domain expertise in security engineering and compliance, paired with the ability to actually build AI-powered tools rather than only prompt existing ones. I know where AI tools tend to fail in this space — inventing controls, assuming facts about an environment, overstepping into judgment calls that should stay with a human — and I design against those failure modes from the start.

That combination of domain judgment and hands-on build capability is what I aim to bring to any team working at the intersection of AI and security.
